# Public Lab audio

The public experience serves its four-part original score cycle from local `/public/audio` assets. Each work is by Scott Buckley and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:

- `the-vision-scott-buckley.mp3` — “The Vision” — https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/the-vision/
- “Decoherence” — https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/decoherence/
- “Computations in a Snowstorm” — https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/computations/
- “Adrift Among Infinite Stars” — https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/adrift-among-infinite-stars/

Required attribution for each work follows the format: `'<title>' by Scott Buckley - released under CC-BY 4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au`.

The system, applications, Lab, simulation, discovery and methodology sections select distinct scores. Audio begins only after the visitor activates the entry gate. The volume control is a 0–100% slider and acts on the active score immediately; preferences persist locally. Score changes use a short crossfade. Audio pauses when the document is hidden and stops on page exit. No previous interface or micro-sound files are played.

Audio playback respects browser autoplay policy, and the complete Lab remains usable with sound off after entry.
