# Public demonstrative models

## MCDA

Normalizes visible weights, calculates dimension contributions, applies a visible risk penalty and evidence adjustment, and returns an uncertainty interval. The formula is demonstrative and not the private scoring system.

## Monte Carlo

Uses a seeded pseudo-random generator and editable distributions to explore uncertain outcomes. It reports mean, median, percentiles, threshold probability, and loss probability. Results inherit the assumptions of their inputs.

## Sensitivity

Re-runs ranking after visible weight and preference changes. It compares prior and current positions and identifies the largest contribution change.

## Bayesian update

Uses prior odds and demonstrative evidence likelihoods to calculate a posterior probability. It does not prove causality or market demand.

## Scenarios, risk, and Pareto

Downside, base, and upside scenarios adjust public parameters. The risk matrix combines probability and impact with visible uncertainty. Pareto comparison identifies non-dominated trade-offs without forcing every decision into one score.
